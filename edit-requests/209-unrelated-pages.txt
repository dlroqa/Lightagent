=== message
Expect evidence from pages unrelated to the query, as ranking gives

Tests only. The trace's third scenario expected no_relevant_evidence when
every page and snippet shares no word with the query. The harness ranks
them anyway: with no lexical match and no semantic endpoint, the search
engine's order stands (store.rs), so the outcome is evidence. The
scenario now records that behaviour. A rag.realtime "No relevant evidence
found" therefore means, in practice, that the search provider returned no
result the parser could read.
