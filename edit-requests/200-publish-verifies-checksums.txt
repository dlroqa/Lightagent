=== message
Let the publish job verify the checksums it attaches, never make them

The shape test forbade any `sha256sum` in the publish job, so it also
caught the new `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` check of the assets against the
dry run's checksums. It now forbids exactly what it meant: computing
checksums in the publish job. Verifying them stays required.
